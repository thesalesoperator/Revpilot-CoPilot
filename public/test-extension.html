📋 What to Watch For

When the extension is working correctly, it should:

  1. Track script progress - Move through sections as conversation progresses
  2. Suggest responses - Tell you what to say based on what prospect said
  3. Detect objections - Recognize when prospect pushes back
  4. Identify buying signals - Alert you when prospect shows interest
